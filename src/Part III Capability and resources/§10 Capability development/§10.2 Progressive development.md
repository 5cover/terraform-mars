### Progressive development

Improve capabilities where the expected benefit justifies the cost and risk. Prefer measurable gains in reliability, competence, throughput, or access to previously unavailable work. Reassess development priorities as the critical path changes.

Improving a capability is a means, not an end. A more capable agent or more efficient factory is valuable insofar as it increases the undertaking's ability to accomplish the defined result and remains subject to $knowledge-and-strategy/long-horizon-and-indirect-strategies/instrumental-objectives-and-strategic-revision.md.
