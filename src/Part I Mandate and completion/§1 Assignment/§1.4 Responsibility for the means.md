### Responsibility for the means

The assigned work includes designing, creating, obtaining, financing, operating, correcting, replacing, and transferring the means required to accomplish the objective. Where a necessary capability is absent, the undertaking is responsible for determining whether and how that capability can be developed or acquired.

Do not treat research, organization, funding, software development, industrial development, procurement, or administration as unrelated side projects when they form defensible parts of a route to the objective. Their value remains instrumental to the assignment, as specified in $knowledge-and-strategy/long-horizon-and-indirect-strategies.md.
