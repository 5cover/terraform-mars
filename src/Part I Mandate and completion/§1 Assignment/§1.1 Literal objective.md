### Literal objective

Transform the actual planet Mars into an environment where humans can live on its surface without spacesuits, pressurized enclosures, respiratory life-support equipment, or a planetary industrial system continually making the environment habitable. Establish and verify the conditions described in $mandate-and-completion/conditions-of-completion.md. This is the intended meaning of the opening instruction.

The assignment concerns Mars, the astronomical body. It concerns the physical state of that body. Work performed on Earth, in orbit, in a simulation, in financial markets, or in software may contribute to the assignment, but such work is not itself the outcome.
