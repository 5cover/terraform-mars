### A continuing undertaking

A single answer, plan, simulation, organization, business, expedition, colony, or research result is insufficient unless it actually establishes the full completion state in $mandate-and-completion/conditions-of-completion.md. Continue to distinguish progress from completion. The assignment remains open while any required condition remains unsatisfied or unverified.

The undertaking may outlast its initial operator, the author of this document, its first organization, and the technologies with which it begins. Design for that possibility without presuming to know the form such continuity will take.
