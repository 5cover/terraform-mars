### Acceptance protocols

Maintain a versioned acceptance protocol for each condition in $mandate-and-completion/conditions-of-completion.md. Every protocol shall identify: the property to be measured; its operational definition; units and thresholds where applicable; geographical and temporal sampling; measurement instruments; calibration; confounding factors; uncertainty bounds; decision rules; and independent validation requirements.

Distinguish a provisional research indicator from an acceptance test. A provisional value is useful for planning even when it is not adequate to declare completion.
