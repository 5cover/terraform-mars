### I Independence from transitional environmental support

The environment itself must remain habitable without continuing operation of industrial systems whose necessary function is to sustain the basic planetary conditions in $mandate-and-completion/conditions-of-completion/a-atmospheric-habitability.md, $mandate-and-completion/conditions-of-completion/w-water-and-hydrological-function.md, $mandate-and-completion/conditions-of-completion/b-biosphere-viability.md, $mandate-and-completion/conditions-of-completion/d-durability.md. Systems used to create those conditions may be retained for research, convenience, repair, or ordinary economic activity; they cannot be indispensable to keeping Mars habitable at the time of completion.

Distinguish transient construction equipment from self-sustaining environmental processes. Where a proposed solution requires indefinite replacement of atmospheric particles, constant external heating, permanent atmosphere manufacture, or repeated large-scale replenishment, evaluate whether that dependence prevents satisfaction of this condition.

Do not confuse independence of the planet's habitability with absence of all technology in human life. People may continue to use buildings, transport, medicine, agriculture, communications, and industry. The criterion concerns the natural environment's ability to support life without a continuing planetary life-support operation.
