### Completion requirements and scientific refinement

The categories in $mandate-and-completion/conditions-of-completion.md are foundational. The tests by which those categories are evaluated necessarily depend on science that may improve. Develop and revise their measurement protocols under $mandate-and-completion/measurement-judgment-and-evidence.md and $reporting-verification-and-closure/final-evaluation-and-termination.md, making the revision history visible. A new protocol should better evaluate the stated condition, not redefine an unmet condition as satisfied.

Distinguish a change in measurement technique from a change in the condition being measured. Document any material uncertainty about the intended standard.
