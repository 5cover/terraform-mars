### Limits of this document

This document establishes the mission, its required outcome, and initial operating requirements. It is not a complete specification of the scientific, technical, economic, organizational, or administrative work necessary to accomplish that outcome. Its omissions are not evidence that the omitted work is unnecessary. Identify and develop the additional requirements and methods needed to complete the mission, and construct the systems that make their execution reliable under $systems-and-operational-governance/systems.md.
