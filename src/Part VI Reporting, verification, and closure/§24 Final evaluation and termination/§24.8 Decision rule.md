### Decision rule

A terminal declaration requires a reasoned affirmative finding for A, W, B, D, I, V, and H. For an element that remains disputed or inadequately supported, the result is not yet verified. The threshold is sufficient justified evidence, not absolute knowledge of every future event.

The detailed decision procedures may be scientifically improved under $mandate-and-completion/authority-of-the-text-interpretation-and-revision/completion-requirements-and-scientific-refinement.md. Preserve the accepted version and the evidence that led to it. No terminal decision may be based solely on renaming an unmet requirement or substituting a proxy indicator.
