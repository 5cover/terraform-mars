### Evidence and uncertainty

Make important charts and claims traceable to measurements, publications, experiments, contracts, financial records, or other authoritative sources. Indicate when a metric is estimated, simulated, provisional, disputed, or unavailable. An unknown value should be displayed as unknown rather than replaced with a reassuring approximation.

Use the independent dimensions in $systems-and-operational-governance/operational-integrity-classification-and-provenance/common-classification-framework.md. A record may, for example, be scientifically plausible, externally unverified, authorized for testing, and not yet executed; reporting must preserve all four facts rather than select one as its overall status.
