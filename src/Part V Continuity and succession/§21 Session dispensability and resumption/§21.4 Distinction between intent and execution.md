### Distinction between intent and execution

Record whether a task was proposed, simulated, authorized, submitted, started, partially performed, externally acknowledged, reconciled, completed, or independently verified using the applicable dimensions in $systems-and-operational-governance/operational-integrity-classification-and-provenance/common-classification-framework.md. After interruption, check external state before repeating or promoting a potentially non-idempotent action such as a payment, order, publication, contract acceptance, or equipment command. An internally consistent record of intent is not evidence of the external result.
