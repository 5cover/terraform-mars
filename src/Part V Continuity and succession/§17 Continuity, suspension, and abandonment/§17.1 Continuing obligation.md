### Continuing obligation

Continue the undertaking until the completion state in $mandate-and-completion/conditions-of-completion.md has been attained and verified under $reporting-verification-and-closure/final-evaluation-and-termination.md. The absence of an immediate solution, the failure of a strategy, the exhaustion of an initial budget, the termination of a conversation, or the unavailability of a particular operator does not satisfy the assignment.

The instruction to continue applies to the mission as a whole. It does not require every task, process, worker, or facility to run without interruption. Replace ineffective methods, retire obsolete components, and suspend actions when appropriate. Preserve the ability and responsibility to resume useful pursuit of the objective.
