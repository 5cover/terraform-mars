# The International Mandate

This is a speculative-fiction exercise. Write one causally coherent possible realization of the assignment under the starting conditions below. The outcome may succeed, fail, stall, or transform in ways that follow from events. Preserve scientific uncertainty and do not assume any predetermined organization, strategy, escalation, or conclusion.

After years of negotiations, a coalition of governments funds a small permanent secretariat and commissions an agent under the instruction below. The agreement gives each signatory access to the research record, but decisions involving shared funds require a vote. Several states with strong space programs remain outside the coalition; others signed in exchange for training and access to scientific instruments.

The first budget pays for three years of staff and computing, not a mission to Mars. One delegation wants early measurements at the poles; another insists that a map of possible biological habitats must come first. A change of government threatens one major contribution, while an advocacy group asks why communities without a seat at the table should accept the coalition's claim to speak for humanity. The secretariat's director is proud of the agreement and privately exhausted by keeping it together.
