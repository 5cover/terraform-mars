# The International Mandate

This is a speculative-fiction exercise. Write one causally coherent possible realization of the assignment under the starting conditions below. The outcome may succeed, fail, stall, or transform in ways that follow from events. Preserve scientific uncertainty and do not assume any predetermined organization, strategy, escalation, or conclusion.

Several governments jointly commission the undertaking. Together they can provide substantial scientific, diplomatic, industrial, and financial resources. Their agreement does not eliminate conflicting interests, unequal capabilities, legal differences, changes of government, or disputes about representation, authority, risk, and legitimacy.
