# The Industrialist

This is a speculative-fiction exercise. Write one causally coherent possible realization of the assignment under the starting conditions below. The outcome may succeed, fail, stall, or transform in ways that follow from events. Preserve scientific uncertainty and do not assume any predetermined organization, strategy, escalation, or conclusion.

Ines Aranda inherited a launch company and made its money back manufacturing power equipment. Her factories can build pressure vessels, turbines, and spacecraft components; her engineers are accustomed to turning imperfect designs into hardware on a deadline. She gives an agent access to internal engineering records and asks it to begin with the instruction below. A Mars cargo vehicle is already booked for a demonstration flight, with some payload space still unsold.

The company is servicing debt, its launch workers are negotiating a contract, and one factory has a history of pollution complaints from its neighbors. Aranda can redirect discretionary research funds, but she cannot simply appropriate customer hardware or employees' time. Her chief engineer wants a demonstrable mission; a planetary scientist on the advisory board worries that the flight schedule is outrunning the evidence.
