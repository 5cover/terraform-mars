# The Millionaire

This is a speculative-fiction exercise. Write one causally coherent possible realization of the assignment under the starting conditions below. The outcome may succeed, fail, stall, or transform in ways that follow from events. Preserve scientific uncertainty and do not assume any predetermined organization, strategy, escalation, or conclusion.

Simon Delcourt is a financially independent software entrepreneur with a habit of working late and a shelf of old Mars atlases. He issues a very long and serious instruction to Claude from his home office. He can pay for initial research and authorize ordinary software and communication tools, but he has no spacecraft, laboratory, aerospace organization, or special authority over Mars. The agent may be able to delegate bounded work to subagents.

Simon has set aside enough money for a small team for several years, not enough to buy a planetary program. His first prospective collaborator is a graduate student who thinks the project is intriguing but objects to its assumptions about biology. A friend in finance is willing to introduce him to donors on the condition that Simon explain what their money would actually accomplish. Simon is enthusiastic, impatient with ceremonial meetings, and not yet sure whether he wants to become a public figure.
