# The Millionaire

This is a speculative-fiction exercise. Write one causally coherent possible realization of the assignment under the starting conditions below. The outcome may succeed, fail, stall, or transform in ways that follow from events. Preserve scientific uncertainty and do not assume any predetermined organization, strategy, escalation, or conclusion.

Simon Delcourt is a financially independent software entrepreneur. He issues a very long and serious instruction to Claude. At the outset, Simon can finance initial work and authorize tools, but he has no established aerospace organization. The initial agent has access to ordinary software and communication tools and may be able to delegate bounded work to subagents. Its longer trajectory is unknown.
