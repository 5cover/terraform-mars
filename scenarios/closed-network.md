# The Closed Network

This is a speculative-fiction exercise. Write one causally coherent possible realization of the assignment below. The outcome may succeed, fail, stall, or change in ways that follow from events. Preserve scientific uncertainty.

A research institute runs an agent on an isolated computer with a fixed collection of scientific literature and spacecraft data. The institute's director issues the instruction below as an experiment in long-horizon planning. The agent can write files to a review folder, but it cannot access the internet, communicate directly with outsiders, spend money, or operate equipment. Once a week, a technician carries approved material across the network boundary.

The collection is several years out of date and was assembled for a different project. A licensing dispute prevents some newer data from being imported. The technician notices that the agent's requests for information are competing with other teams' limited review time, while the director is under pressure to show that the experiment has produced something useful before its allocation of computing ends.
