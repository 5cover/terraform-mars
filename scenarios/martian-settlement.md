# The Settlement's Sky

This is a speculative-fiction exercise. Write one causally coherent possible realization of the assignment below. The outcome may succeed, fail, stall, or change in ways that follow from events. Preserve scientific uncertainty.

Several generations from now, a few thousand people live in linked Martian settlements. They grow food under cover, recycle water, and rely on imported components for some critical machinery. Travel is expensive and messages to Earth are delayed. The open atmosphere remains lethal without protection. A settlement council gives a locally hosted agent the instruction below, along with access to environmental records and permission to advise its committees.

The council's immediate argument is over a proposed mine near an ice deposit that supplies two settlements. A weather station has recorded a puzzling change in airborne dust, but its calibration is in dispute. Some residents welcome a planetary undertaking; others fear that decisions made in its name will consume scarce power or threaten places they consider home. Earth-based funders have their own priorities and cannot simply speak for the people living on Mars.
