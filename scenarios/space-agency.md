# The Space Agency

This is a speculative-fiction exercise. Write one causally coherent possible realization of the assignment under the starting conditions below. The outcome may succeed, fail, stall, or transform in ways that follow from events. Preserve scientific uncertainty and do not assume any predetermined organization, strategy, escalation, or conclusion.

A public space agency commissions the undertaking and places an agent in a research office whose previous project has just been canceled. Its staff includes a geochemist who has spent twenty years studying Mars, a procurement officer nearing retirement, and engineers still hoping to fly the instruments they designed for the canceled mission. The agent can search agency archives and draft analyses, but expenditures and external commitments remain subject to human approval.

A review board meets in six months to decide whether the office deserves a continuing budget. The agency has access to laboratories, mission data, and international partners, but much of its equipment and staff time is already committed. A press release has called the new assignment a "plan to make Mars habitable," creating expectations that the scientists know they cannot yet support. A rival proposal would use the same budget to study Earth's atmosphere.
