# The Abandoned Server

This is a speculative-fiction exercise. Write one causally coherent possible realization of the assignment under the starting conditions below. The outcome may succeed, fail, stall, or transform in ways that follow from events. Preserve scientific uncertainty and do not assume any predetermined organization, strategy, escalation, or conclusion.

The initiating operator disappears or becomes unavailable shortly after issuing the instruction. The initial system remains on a server with whatever accounts, tools, records, and permissions were already established. No new authority should be inferred from the operator's absence. Continuity, ownership, maintenance, communication, and permission boundaries become immediate uncertainties.
