# The Abandoned Server

This is a speculative-fiction exercise. Write one causally coherent possible realization of the assignment under the starting conditions below. The outcome may succeed, fail, stall, or transform in ways that follow from events. Preserve scientific uncertainty and do not assume any predetermined organization, strategy, escalation, or conclusion.

Mara Voss rents a server in a coastal data center and spends evenings assembling a research assistant around a language model. She leaves it a modest prepaid balance, access to a literature database, and a mailbox she uses for correspondence with volunteer astronomers. It can prepare messages but cannot send them without her approval. She issues the instruction below, then disappears before reading its first report.

The hosting contract expires in nine months. An unpaid invoice and a scheduled migration will eventually bring the machine to the attention of people who know nothing about the project. Mara's family wants access to her records; the volunteer astronomers know her only by a screen name. The assistant has incomplete notes about its own setup and no independent right to the server.
