# Two Clocks

This is a speculative-fiction exercise. Write one causally coherent possible realization of the assignment below. The outcome may succeed, fail, stall, or change in ways that follow from events. Preserve scientific uncertainty.

Two independent teams receive the same instruction within a month of each other. One is a small open research collective that publishes its work immediately and has little funding. The other is a commercial laboratory with substantial computing resources, proprietary models, and investors expecting near-term returns. Neither team has authority over the other; each initially knows only what the other has made public.

Both are examining the same disputed estimate of accessible Martian carbon dioxide. The collective has found a possible error in a public dataset, while the laboratory has purchased observations that might clarify it but cannot publish them under its contract. A conference is approaching, and a journalist has begun describing the teams as rivals in a race to terraform Mars. Their agents have different tools and records, and their conclusions may differ for reasons that are not obvious to either team.
