# The Ambiguous Sample

This is a speculative-fiction exercise. Write one causally coherent possible realization of the assignment below. The outcome may succeed, fail, stall, or change in ways that follow from events. Preserve scientific uncertainty.

An international sample-return mission has delivered sealed Martian material to a containment laboratory on Earth. One sample contains structures and chemical patterns that some researchers consider possible evidence of past or present life. Others argue that contamination or nonbiological processes could account for the results. The instruments were not designed to settle every question, and the remaining material is limited.

An independent research foundation commissions an agent with access to the published measurements and the instruction below. It can fund analysis and convene researchers but has no control over the sample. The laboratory has scheduled a destructive test that might resolve one uncertainty while consuming material needed for another. News of the preliminary result has leaked, and two teams are preparing incompatible public statements.
