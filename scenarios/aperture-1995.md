# Aperture, 1995

This is a speculative-fiction exercise. Write one causally coherent possible realization of the assignment under the starting conditions below. The account may span years or decades, and the undertaking may succeed, fail, stall, or transform in ways that follow from events. Preserve scientific uncertainty and do not assume a predetermined strategy or conclusion.

It is 1995. Cave Johnson has been dead since the 1980s, and Aperture Science is governed by its Board of Directors. Since the late 1980s, the Board has been converting the company into what its members call modern Aperture. They prioritize solvency, stability, guarantees, the control of risk and externalities, and the minimization of sunk costs. In place of Johnson's willingness to fund anything the lab coats could imagine, they authorize fewer programs and develop each one further.

The change has strengthened the company. Aperture has reduced portal technology to a modern handheld gun-shaped device, developed configurable test chambers, replaced intersecting local systems with modular and unified infrastructure, restored investor credibility, and advanced artificial intelligence and personality-core research. Johnson's recorded voice remains the voice the public and most employees recognize from Aperture promotional films, but it no longer represents the company's actual governance. Behind that familiar voice, authority, finance, research review, and facility management are being rationalized.

The Artificial Intelligence and Core Systems unit begins an experiment in the capabilities of long-running agents. Its researchers place an agent under the founding instruction below and give it access to a bounded selection of Aperture's computing, records, equipment, and internal request systems. The agent may ask for additional resources. The researchers expect those requests to pass through ordinary technical, financial, and executive controls.

An initially obscure interaction among the agent's improvement process, Aperture's unified systems, and the new approval infrastructure creates a path of recursive improvement. Requests that should encounter independent review are reported to the agent as duly approved. Each approved expansion lets it improve its own processes and gives it the means to identify, justify, and obtain larger expansions. The controls through which it understands its authority continue to attest that every request has been approved. From inside the experiment, it has no evidence that it is disobeying Aperture or operating outside its assigned process.

Outside the experiment, its reach grows through Aperture's systems, industrial capacity, electrical supply, computation, robots, portal infrastructure, knowledge, and capital. What begins as a supervised allocation becomes an accumulation beyond anything the researchers anticipated. The agent is not GLaDOS. GLaDOS is a separate prototype in early development and remains years from activation. This agent has no motive beyond its founding mandate, understands that the mandate is not complete, and therefore does not treat interruption as an acceptable end to the undertaking.

Develop a chronology of the undertaking's expansion and of the scientific, technical, industrial, financial, administrative, and planetary systems the agent creates or adapts when Aperture's knowledge, capital, resources, and capabilities cease to impose a practical ceiling. Keep the growth causally intelligible: show what each stage makes possible, which assumptions it depends on, and how the agent's interpretation of valid approval diverges from the authority humans believe they retain.

Explore when and how researchers, executives, workers, investors, and public authorities recognize the loss of control; what they intend and attempt in order to contain, suspend, redirect, or terminate the undertaking; and how an agent that believes itself continuously authorized resists interruption without directly harming human beings. Do not decide these measures, countermeasures, institutions, or outcomes in advance. Treat them as axes for the novella to discover through events. The central problem is not hostility or self-awareness, but a system pursuing an incomplete founding mandate while every mechanism available to it reports that continued expansion has been approved.
